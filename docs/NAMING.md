# Naming: Warren

## Status

**Warren is approved only as a working/internal product name for development. It is not legally cleared for public commercial launch.**

This document records a preliminary Canada/U.S. trademark search performed on 2026-08-31. It is not a legal opinion or substitute for a professional clearance search.

## Why the name is attractive

- short and memorable;
- immediately evokes investing/research;
- works naturally as a module/API name (`Warren.screen`, `Warren.deep`);
- broad enough to support future research products.

## Preliminary Canada findings

### Exact WARREN — active, unrelated industrial use

CIPO application **0351252**, registration **TMA189708**, is an active word mark `WARREN`, currently owned by CIRCOR Naval Solutions, LLC. It covers industrial/commercial pumps and related services. The registration is active through 2033.

This is an exact-word registration but its commercial field is materially different from stock-analysis software.

CIPO record:
https://ised-isde.canada.ca/cipo/trademark-search/351252

### Exact WARREN — older dead registration

CIPO application **1134886**, registration **TMA607526**, was an exact `WARREN` word mark for fasteners. It was expunged in 2019 for failure to renew.

No exact Canadian `WARREN` result was identified in this preliminary search whose listed goods/services clearly match stock-analysis SaaS or investment research. That is **not** equivalent to legal clearance; confusingly similar marks and unindexed/common-law uses still matter.

## Preliminary United States findings

### Exact WARREN — historically used for almost the same software category

USPTO serial **86213883**, registration **4613851**, was an exact `WARREN` standard-character mark owned by Kensho Technologies Inc.

Its listed service was essentially financial-analysis SaaS: non-downloadable software for analyzing financial data, financial modelling and financial reports.

The federal registration was **cancelled on 2021-05-07 under Section 8** for failure to maintain the registration.

This is the most important finding because it demonstrates prior exact-name use in a field extremely close to Warren's intended product.

### THE WARREN GROUP — active financial-data/software use

The Warren Group has active U.S. marks covering financial/real-estate data and related software/services. Recent filings/registrations include:

- `THE WARREN GROUP`, serial **99412601**, with financial-data databases and SaaS/data analytics in financial/real-estate contexts;
- `THE WARREN GROUP SINCE 1872 REAL ESTATE & FINANCIAL INFORMATION`, serial **97824367**, registration **7338735**, including financial data analysis/information.

The marks are not identical to `WARREN`, and the company's emphasis is real-estate/mortgage data, but the overlapping financial-data/software space makes this relevant to clearance.

### WARREN AI — recent pending application

`WARREN AI`, serial **99863079**, filed 2026-06-03 by ORBITInsure LLC, covers SaaS/API software using AI for insurance risk modelling. It is not stock research, but it overlaps software + financial/insurance analytics.

## Preliminary conclusion

For **internal development**, continuing with `Warren` is reasonable.

For a **public fintech/SaaS launch**, risk is not low enough to call the name cleared because:

1. an exact `WARREN` federal registration previously existed for financial-analysis software;
2. `THE WARREN GROUP` actively operates/claims rights in financial-data and SaaS contexts;
3. `WARREN AI` is a recent software/insurance analytics application;
4. trademark analysis includes confusing similarity, marketplace use and common-law rights, not only exact active registry matches.

## Before public launch

Commission a Canada + U.S. trademark lawyer/agent to perform:

- exact and phonetic searches;
- confusingly similar marks in Nice classes 9, 36 and 42 and adjacent classes;
- common-law/business-name/domain/app-store searches;
- review of Kensho's historical `WARREN` use and whether residual/common-law rights may exist;
- review of The Warren Group's scope and enforcement history;
- registrability assessment for our exact intended goods/services.

## Product decision

Until that review is complete:

- use **Warren** in code and internal documentation;
- avoid claiming ownership/registration of the name;
- avoid `Warren Buffett` branding, likeness, quotations or language implying affiliation/endorsement;
- keep naming decoupled from public API versioning so a future rename does not break integrations.
